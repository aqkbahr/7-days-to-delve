geometry and animation assets were not exported or redistributed.

physics bodies, sounds, AI, root motion and damage logic. Those native game
assets remain subject to the game's own terms. This record does not grant
rights to redistribute any native game asset.

Material: one atlas material, opaque, matte, no realtime per-creature lights.
